Left is the budget minus what has been spent; negative means over budget. It is green when spending is at or under pace, amber up to 10% over, light red from 10% over, dark red from 30% over. Rent, Utilities and Fitness are judged against the full month; everything else against the day of the month of the latest transaction. The grey figure is the share of the month's budget still left. Discretionary categories share one budget, so they show what each has spent.
